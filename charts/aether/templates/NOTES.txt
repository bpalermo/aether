UPGRADE ORDER (issue #815, applies to chart 0.92.28 and newer):
  Do NOT jump to this chart from a release OLDER than 0.92.27. Upgrade to
  0.92.27 first, let every node's aether-proxy take the new listeners, then
  upgrade again. 0.92.27 taught each pod's listener to stamp its SPIFFE ID
  into filter state; this release moved the outbound client-certificate
  selection onto that key. Skipping 0.92.27 leaves a window in which a pod's
  egress presents the NODE identity instead of its own, because LDS and CDS
  are separate xDS responses with no ordering guarantee. Not a hard failure,
  but visible to inbound RBAC / ext_authz / peer-identity checks. Already on
  0.92.27 or later, or a fresh install: nothing to do.
  Verify afterwards -- this must stay flat at 0 on every node during a pod
  roll, at a 5s step:
    max_over_time(envoy_cluster_manager_warming_clusters[1m])
  See docs/runbook.md, "#815 release two".
{{- end }}
